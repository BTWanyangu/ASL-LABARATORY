# Expanded service scope & research references

Research performed 12 October 2026. Valcambi's public website is used **only to identify service categories and general educational topics**; it is not an ASL partner, accreditor or source of ASL operational claims. Text on this site is original editorial copy, not reproduced Valcambi service-page text.

## Seven source service categories

| Category (Valcambi navigation) | ASL route | Editorial approach |
|---|---|---|
| Transportation | `/services/transportation/` | Shipment planning, documentation, handover, security and insurance considerations |
| Reception | `/services/reception/` | Item intake, checks, identity and traceability |
| Assaying | `/services/assaying/` | Precious-metal assaying concepts, sampling and the meaning of analytical findings |
| Refining | `/services/refining/` | Feedstock information, technical route selection and material balance |
| Manufacturing | `/services/manufacturing/` | Product form, specification, provenance records and contractual production considerations |
| Financing | `/services/financing/` | Transaction structure, independent verification and regulatory eligibility, **not** a loan application |
| Storage | `/services/storage/` | Identification, custody, security, insurance and authorised release questions |

The ASL site **also** includes `mineral-buying` and `mineral-trading`, as explicitly requested by the owner. These have a dedicated `/trade/` overview in addition to their service detail routes. Six preexisting services remain: minerals, gold, diamonds, gemstones, sample handling and reporting, for a total of **15 individual service routes**.

## Important business confirmation before publication

The logo and original project brief confirm ASL's **assay-oriented branding**, but do not establish that it operates a refinery, foundry, vault, regulated financing programme, high-security transport company, purchasing desk, or mineral trading licence. The added pages are informative *service enquiries*, not representations of such achieved facilities or authorisations. The owner should verify actual operational scope, licences, partners, geographic markets, inspection standards, purchase authority and acceptance criteria before presenting any service as confirmed. If a category is not in ASL's actual business scope, remove it from the `SERVICES` content source and `SERVICE_GROUPS` in `content/expanded_services.py` before public launch.

The site does not invent prices, acceptance guarantees, vault security claims, guaranteed funding, seller listings, real shipment records, transaction execution or certificates. Enquiry text is prepared locally and must be sent by the user through their email application when a verified email address is configured. No data is transmitted automatically.
